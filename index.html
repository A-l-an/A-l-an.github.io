About the project

Why it matters

Mobile tasks involve several decisions across different screens. For blind and low vision users, screen readers turn a structured interface into a long sequence of spoken items. Relevant choices are mixed with unrelated content, while details needed for comparison are scattered. This creates extra mental effort and can lead people to miss information that matters to their decision.

How FocalFlow helps

FocalFlow uses LLM agents to organise interface information around the user’s current task. It provides a task overview, filters the screen reader’s focus to relevant options without moving their positions, and reveals supporting details when the user asks. This helps users keep track of the task and explore alternatives while retaining control over the final choice.
